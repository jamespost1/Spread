Same product, or just a similar one?

That question is the entire problem. These two titles are 88% identical and share every meaningful word — and are different products, a hundred dollars apart: 

Apple Watch Series 9 GPS 45mm Midnight Aluminum
Apple Watch Series 8 GPS 45mm Midnight Aluminum Case

These two share almost no wording, and are the same headphones:

Sony WH-1000XM5 Wireless Noise Canceling Headphones
Sony WH1000XM5 Over-Ear Bluetooth Headphone, Black

Spread runs a two-stage matcher. A deterministic scorer settles the clear cases instantly and for free; only genuinely ambiguous pairs go to a language model, which confirms whether they describe the same item. Fuzzy similarity is allowed to reject a match but never to confirm one — that rule took measured match precision from 50% to 100%.

Prices come from first-party retailer APIs, not scraped search results. Amazon and Target publish no open product API, so they work as source pages rather than comparison targets.
