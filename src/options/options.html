Spread

Real prices across retailers. No account, no setup.

You're all set

Open a product page on Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, eBay or Costco and look for the Compare price button next to the price. There is nothing to configure.

Your data

Spread sends the title, price, brand and model of a product to its comparison service only when you click Compare price. It never sends your browsing history, and it has no account system.

Your savings totals are stored on this device only. Clear them any time.

Advanced

Only change this if you are running your own Spread Worker. Leave it blank for the default.
